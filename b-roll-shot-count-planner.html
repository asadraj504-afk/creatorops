B-Roll Shot Count Planner

Turn a target runtime into a practical B-roll workload before you start sourcing or generating visuals.

PLANNING GUIDE

How many B-roll shots do you need?

The planner divides your usable visual timeline by the average time each shot stays on screen. It then splits the result between video clips and still images.

Use it before sourcing assets

A shot-count target helps documentary, faceless and explainer creators estimate how much footage, image generation or stock research a project may require.

Planning estimate, not an editing rule

Real edits mix short and long shots. Use the result as a workload target, then adjust pacing around important moments.
