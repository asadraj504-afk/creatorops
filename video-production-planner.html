HOW IT WORKS

Plan your video before production starts

CreatorOps estimates narration words from speaking pace, then converts your usable timeline into visual beats and splits those beats into footage and image requirements.

What is a visual beat?

A visual beat is one planned visual change on the timeline. If your visuals change every 4 seconds, a 20-minute timeline needs roughly 300 visual beats before exclusions.

Are the word counts exact?

No. They are planning estimates because real speaking speed, pauses and editing rhythm vary.

Does CreatorOps upload my inputs?

Not in V1. The calculator logic runs in your browser.
