Editing is only one production cost. Research, scripting, voiceover, thumbnails, stock or AI assets, subscriptions and revisions can materially change the real cost of publishing.

Why separate per-video and monthly costs?

Freelancer and asset costs usually scale with output, while subscriptions are often fixed. Separating them makes it easier to compare publishing frequencies.
